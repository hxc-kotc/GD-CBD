# Literature check notes, 2026-10-09

**Provenance:** second-hand. Six standard web searches; only titles, URLs and search-engine summaries were seen. No full text was read (paper hosts are blocked in the cloud sandbox). Doses, vehicles and timings below come from summaries and must be checked in the papers before citing.

## Findings

### CBD in incision pain (other labs)
- PMC12844942, dorsum incision, male Sprague-Dawley rats: CBD 3, 10 or 30 mg/kg IP given on post-surgery day 8; 3 mg/kg partially attenuated primary mechanical hypersensitivity, site- and dose-specific; no effect on secondary hypersensitivity. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12844942/
- Behavioural Brain Research 2023, plantar incision, Wistar males and females: 3 mg/kg reduced allodynia in males; in females the effective dose depended on estrous phase (0.3 mg/kg in late diestrus, slower onset). Seen via IASP summary: https://www.iasp-pain.org/publications/pain-research-forum/papers-of-the-week/paper/antinociceptive-action-of-cannabidiol-on-thermal-sensitivity-and-post-operative-pain-in-male-and-female-rats/
- Frontiers in Pharmacology 2017, Wistar rats: IP CBD 3 and 10 mg/kg reduced incision mechanical allodynia; affective dimension via conditioned place preference. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28680401/

### Dendrimer-CBD
- No peer-reviewed dendrimer-CBD pain paper found. The only GD-CBD trace is the lab's own conference slides (Controlled Release Society archive: https://www.controlledreleasesociety.org/sites/default/files/images/2025/Archive%20PPTs/3088321.pdf) reporting 12.5% loading, about 6 nm, five days of reduced post-operative pain sensitivity, and a filed patent. Slides, not peer review.
- Non-dendrimer CBD carriers: polymeric micelles (oral, chemical pain model), PMC10254120.

### Glucose and hydroxyl dendrimer targeting
- Hydroxyl dendrimers: reactive microglia and macrophages (rabbit cerebral palsy; Kannan lab materials). Glucose dendrimer GD2: hyperexcitable neurons (Sharma et al. 2024, Bioeng Transl Med, doi 10.1002/btm2.10655, epilepsy). No peer-reviewed pain-model or DRG biodistribution found; lab slides state "more than 50 models including pain".

### CBD oxidation
- HU-331 forms readily from CBD and is a common impurity in CBD isolates; photo-isomerizes to a reactive intermediate; light-aged solutions color. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10147596/
- Plant-derived vs synthetic CBD differ in trace impurity profile; storage under stress adds HU-331 and cannabielsoin (Sci Rep 2022, https://doaj.org/article/5bcfd09bf118470cb44eb441347da9fd).
- Cayman sells an HU-331 analytical standard, light sensitive, store at -80 C: https://www.caymanchem.com/product/10005673/hu-331
- Not found: published batch-to-batch variability data for research-grade CBD.

### Reviews and statistics
- IASP narrative review of preclinical cannabinoid pain studies (Pain, 2021): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33729211/. Companion meta-analysis not retrieved. No CBD reproducibility or sex-stratified meta-analysis found.
- Von Frey: Chaplan et al. 1994 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7990513/); Christensen et al. 2016 online algorithm for more accurate 50% thresholds (https://research.regionh.dk/da/publications/von-frey-testing-revisited-provision-of-an-online-algorithm-for-i/). Nothing retrieved on AUC or mixed-model analysis of von Frey data.
