**Pattern across the full record:** the effect replicated in the two cohorts that share the earliest preparation (V12, V13) and did not reproduce in three later cohorts at the same 3 mg/kg dose (V20, V22 72h, V23), one of which (V23) moved in the opposite direction. Standard chemical characterization has not distinguished the cohorts that worked from the ones that didn't — see the main plan's evidence ledger.
